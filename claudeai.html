What makes Stratum different

Built around how you actually think

Six principles, each designed to eliminate the invisible friction that fragments your day and scatters your attention.

01
Focused time blocks

Schedule deep work sessions that protect your most cognitively demanding tasks from interruption. Stratum learns your peak hours and plans around them.

02
Frictionless capture

Capture tasks, ideas, and notes in under two seconds. Stratum's quick-entry bar is always one keystroke away — no context switch required.

03
Momentum tracking

Visualise streaks, completion rates, and flow patterns over time. Understand exactly where your energy goes — and where to reclaim it.

04
Distraction shield

A single toggle that mutes notifications, hides your inbox, and creates a distraction-free workspace. One click to enter your zone.

05
Smart task linking

Connect related tasks across projects into coherent threads. Stratum surfaces dependencies so you always know what to tackle first.

06
Universal integrations

Connects natively with Notion, Linear, Slack, GitHub, and your calendar. Stratum meets your stack, not the other way around.

The process

From chaos to clarity in four steps

01
Capture everything

Dump every task, idea, and obligation into Stratum's frictionless inbox. No structure needed — just capture.

02
Clarify intent

Each item gets a clear outcome and a realistic time estimate. Stratum helps you ask: what does "done" actually look like?

03
Block your day

Assign tasks to focused time blocks. Stratum maps your energy curve and places demanding work in your peak hours.

04
Work and reflect

Execute with your distraction shield active. Review what you completed each evening to close the loop and reset.
