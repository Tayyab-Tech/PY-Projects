FAQ
FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Who can apply to Mindverse360° programs? +
Our programs are open to university students, recent graduates, early-stage founders, and professionals looking to transition into entrepreneurship. We welcome applicants from all backgrounds — technical or non-technical. What matters most is ambition, coachability, and a genuine desire to build.
How much do the programs cost? +
Program fees vary. The Startup Fundamentals online course is free. The Founder Accelerator and Leadership Track have tuition fees, but we offer scholarships, income share agreements, and corporate-sponsored seats. We believe cost should never be a barrier to the right candidates.
Do I need a business idea to apply? +
Not necessarily. For most programs, you just need the desire to build something. The Founder Accelerator helps you develop and validate your idea from scratch. However, the Startup Studio requires at least an early-stage concept or MVP.
Are programs available outside of Lahore? +
Yes! We run programs in Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad. Several of our programs are also available online or in a hybrid format for participants across Pakistan. We plan to expand to Peshawar and Quetta in 2025.
Does Mindverse360° take equity in my startup? +
Only in the Startup Studio model, where we co-build ventures and take equity in exchange for significant resources, team support, and capital. All other programs — including the Founder Accelerator — do not take any equity. Your startup belongs to you.
How competitive is the application process? +
It's selective but accessible. We typically accept 20–30 participants per cohort and receive 3–5x more applications. We review holistically — not just academic background, but your potential, mindset, and fit with our cohort culture.
