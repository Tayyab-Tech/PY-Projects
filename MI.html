The Model

Ecosystem Overview

A fully integrated pathway — from technical talent to market-ready founders — built specifically for Pakistan.

How It Works

Five Stages. One Journey.
Infinite Potential.

Each stage is designed to be sequential but flexible. Participants can enter at any stage based on their current position in the founder journey.

🎓
Stage 01 — Discovery

Identify & Activate Talent

We partner with universities and institutions to identify high-potential students, running assessment programs and talent discovery events across Pakistan.

🧠
Stage 02 — Learning

Structured Skill Development

Cohort-based programs in entrepreneurship, product management, leadership, and technology — designed and delivered by practitioners, not academics alone.

🔨
Stage 03 — Building

Real-World Execution

Participants join builder teams to solve real industry problems. They build products, gain clients, and develop execution capabilities in a supported environment.

🚀
Stage 04 — Venture

Startup Launch & Support

The best builders graduate to the venture program — with access to co-founders, pre-seed funding, legal structure, investor networks, and go-to-market support.

🌍
Stage 05 — Scale

Global Expansion

Funded, growing startups get access to international networks, regional expansion support, and continued mentorship from global operators and investors.
